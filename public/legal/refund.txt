YOGTATVA REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY
Last Updated: July 13, 2026

1. PLAN DETAILS AND SUBSCRIPTIONS
1.1. YogTatva may offer recurring subscription tiers ("Premium Plans") billed on a Monthly or Annual cycle.
1.2. Currency: All subscription transactions are processed in Indian Rupees (INR) for users in India, or in the equivalent local currency for international users.
1.3. Auto-Renewal: Premium Plans are set to automatically renew at the end of each billing cycle (every 30 days for monthly plans, or every 365 days for annual plans) using the payment method on file, unless you cancel your subscription.

2. CANCELLATION PROCESS
2.1. You can cancel your subscription at any time.
2.2. To cancel:
- Go to the Settings page in the Platform dashboard;
- Select the "Subscription" tab and click "Cancel Subscription";
- Alternatively, if you subscribed via Google Play Store or Apple App Store, you must cancel directly through the subscription management portal of your respective mobile operating system.
2.3. Cancellation Timing: You must cancel your subscription at least twenty-four (24) hours before the renewal date to avoid being billed for the next billing cycle.

3. REFUND ELIGIBILITY AND TRIAL PERIODS
3.1. General Rule: ALL PAYMENTS FOR PREMIUM SUBSCRIPTIONS ARE NON-REFUNDABLE.
3.2. Trial Periods: If YogTatva offers a free trial period, you will not be charged until the trial ends. If you do not wish to transition to a paid plan, you must cancel the subscription before the trial period expires.
3.3. No Partial Refunds: We do not offer partial refunds, prorated credits, or refunds for unused portions of a billing cycle. Following cancellation, you will continue to have access to Premium features until the end of your current active billing period.

4. BILLING DISPUTES AND FAILED PAYMENTS
4.1. Failed Payments: If a subscription renewal payment fails, we reserve the right to suspend your access to Premium features. We will retry processing the payment over a period of seven (7) days before terminating your subscription.
4.2. Billing Disputes: If you believe you have been billed in error or have a pricing dispute, you must notify our billing support team at billing@yogtatva.com within fourteen (14) days of the transaction date.

5. TAXES AND INVOICING
All fees listed on the Platform are exclusive of applicable taxes (including Goods and Services Tax / GST in India), which will be added at checkout and detailed on your invoice in compliance with local taxation laws.
