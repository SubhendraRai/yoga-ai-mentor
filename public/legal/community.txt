YOGTATVA COMMUNITY GUIDELINES
Last Updated: July 13, 2026

1. PURPOSE OF COMMUNITY GUIDELINES
1.1. YogTatva aims to provide a safe, mindful, and encouraging space for users to practice yoga, improve wellness, and track health habits.
1.2. These guidelines apply to all text inputs you submit to the AI Chat Mentor, community discussion forums (if applicable), journal inputs, and user-generated feedback.
1.3. By using the Platform, you agree to uphold these standards.

2. RESPECTFUL AND Mindful COMMUNICATION
We maintain zero tolerance for toxic behavior. You must not submit content that contains:
- Harassment: Direct threats of violence, stalking, trolling, or targeted insults;
- Hate Speech: Content that attacks, demeans, or incites hatred against groups based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or age;
- Vulgarity: Explicit sexual language, extreme profanity, or graphic depictions of violence.

3. PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES
3.1. Spam and Fake Accounts: Do not create duplicate mock accounts to spam the AI servers, scrape data, or manipulate the wellness system logs.
3.2. Impersonation: Do not represent yourself as another person, physical trainer, health professional, or celebrity.
3.3. Medical Misinformation: Do not use the Platform to post or request fraudulent wellness advice, write fake medical logs, or claim that AI yoga flows cured severe medical conditions.
3.4. AI Misuse: Do not attempt to jailbreak the AI Mentor, bypass constraints, use the Groq Llama API for unauthorized purposes, or submit queries intended to generate malware, hate speech, or abuse.

4. COPYRIGHT AND TRADEMARK PROTECTION
Ensure that any text, profiles, or graphics you upload do not violate the intellectual property rights of others.

5. REPORTING ABUSE AND SECURITY BUGS
If you observe users violating these guidelines, or if you identify a security bug in the Supabase backend, please report it immediately to security@yogtatva.com.

6. ENFORCEMENT AND DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS
We reserve the right to investigate violations and take appropriate disciplinary action:
- Account Suspension: Temporary lockout of your account for 7 to 30 days;
- Permanent Ban: Permanent deletion of your account and block of your IP address from Vercel servers;
- Legal Action: Reporting malicious hacks or data breaches to relevant cybersecurity authorities in India (e.g. CERT-In).

7. APPEALS PROCESS
If you believe your account was suspended or banned in error, you may file an appeal by emailing support@yogtatva.com with a statement justifying the request.
